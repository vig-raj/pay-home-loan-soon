# Home Loan Pre-payment Strategy Calculator

> Banks have great calculators to help you enter a loan, but almost no tools to help you exit one early.

## The Story

As a first-time home buyer who purchased during the pandemic, I realized that taking the loan is the easy part. The real challenge is planning repayment in a way that reduces total interest.

Most banking portals offer basic EMI calculators but rarely provide strong **pre-payment simulators**. I wanted answers to questions like:

- **If I increase my EMI by 10 percent next year, how much tenure do I save**
- **If I put my annual bonus into the principal every March, when will I be debt-free**

I was tired of Excel sheets that broke whenever I changed a variable, so I built this simulation tool.

## Features

- **Pre-payment simulation**: Shows the impact of extra payments on the loan tenure.
- **Flexible strategy testing**: Compare one-time lump sums and recurring monthly extras.
- **Tenure vs EMI comparison**: Understand how reducing tenure differs from reducing monthly burden.
- **Charts and visuals**: Clear breakdowns of principal and interest over time.
- **Privacy first**: Everything runs locally in your browser. No data leaves your machine.

## How to Use

1. **Enter loan details**: Principal, interest rate and tenure.
2. **Add pre-payments**: Choose between one-time payments or recurring monthly extras.
3. **Analyze**: The dashboard updates instantly to show interest saved and tenure reduced.

## Disclaimer

**Important**: This tool is for educational and simulation purposes only.

- Bank policies on pre-payment charges differ.
- Floating interest rates are not auto-simulated and must be updated manually.
- Always consult your bank or a certified financial planner before making financial decisions.
